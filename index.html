I'm a passionate Computer Science and ICT teacher with a BS in Information Technology and ongoing Master in Information Technology studies in the dissertation phase.

I currently teach Computer Science at Somtawin Witaedsuksa Huaymongkhon School in Hua Hin — Cambridge for primary and upper secondary, and the Western Australia curriculum for lower secondary. Before that, I was IT / EP Program Head at Holy Child College of Davao, a college IT instructor at St. John Paul II College of Davao, University of Mindanao and Jose Maria College, and a Minecraft Education teacher for a Singapore-based program online. Earlier, I worked as an IT Developer at R.P. Real Estate Corp. and as a Layout Artist.

As a Virtual Assistant, I help business owners with data management, CRM and email support, web content updates, research, social media assistance, Canva design and customer support — streamlining workflows, not just completing tasks.

I speak English and Filipino, and I work remotely from Hua Hin with clients and students worldwide.
